Matthew Ross Meakin

Matthew Ross Meakin (also Mateo; b. Los Angeles) is an American founder and self‑taught engineer. He is the founder and CEO of Basemate[2], a messaging network for stablecoins on Base, currently in Circuit Accelerator and previously Base Batches 002, Coinbase's onchain accelerator.[3] He is also founder & CTO of Beora Care[1], a HIPAA‑compliant clinical platform for behavioral health. He entered addiction treatment as a patient on scholarship after homelessness on Skid Row, taught himself to code, and became the facility's CTO.
